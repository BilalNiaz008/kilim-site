One activation, no expiry

Your key has one activation slot, bound to a PC and Windows user. To transfer it, choose Deactivate this PC from the tray or release the old device in Polar before activating another. The key has no expiry.

Internet is required at activation, every launch and licence checks every 30 minutes. If a check cannot confirm the licence, Kilim closes; reconnect and reopen to retry.
